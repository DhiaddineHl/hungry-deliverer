On the road with Hungry.

The rider app rebuilt on the customer design system: navy actions, orange only for signals (status dot, busy zones, step progress, the current day), the same fields, chips, lists and sheets. Map screens keep one bottom sheet that always shows the current step, who to contact and the one next action.

D1, D2, D10

Going online.

One switch. Go online is the only primary action when offline; the vehicle in use is shown so it can be changed before starting.

Busy areas. Zones are soft orange areas with a label, not dark tooltips over the map. The nearest one sits in the sheet with a Navigate button.

Menu. Today’s earnings and trips at the top, then the same list rows as the customer profile.
