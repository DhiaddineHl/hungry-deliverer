# Administrative Divisions of Tunisia (Regions & Subregions)

This file is structured for machine/AI readability. Tunisia's administrative hierarchy has three main levels:

1. **Macro-region (Grand Region)** — 6 informal statistical groupings used for regional planning (not official admin units).
2. **Governorate (Wilaya / "Region")** — 24 official first-level administrative divisions.
3. **Delegation (Mu'tamadiya / "Subregion")** — 264+ second-level administrative divisions within each governorate.

Each governorate entry below includes its **limits** (bordering governorates, countries, and coastlines) so relationships between regions are clear.

---

## 1. Macro-Regions (6 statistical groupings)

| Macro-Region | Governorates included |
|---|---|
| **North East** | Tunis, Ariana, Ben Arous, Manouba, Bizerte, Nabeul, Zaghouan |
| **North West** | Béja, Jendouba, Kef, Siliana |
| **Centre East** | Sousse, Monastir, Mahdia, Sfax |
| **Centre West** | Kairouan, Kasserine, Sidi Bouzid |
| **South East** | Gabès, Medenine, Tataouine |
| **South West** | Gafsa, Tozeur, Kebili |

General national limits: Tunisia is bordered by **Algeria** to the west/southwest, **Libya** to the southeast, and the **Mediterranean Sea** to the north and east.

## 2. Governorates (Regions) — Details, Limits, and Delegations (Subregions)

### North East

#### Tunis
- **Capital:** Tunis
- **Limits:** Borders Ariana (N), Manouba (W), Ben Arous (S); Gulf of Tunis / Mediterranean Sea (E)
- **Delegations:** Bab El Bhar, Bab Souika, Carthage, Cité El Khadra, Djebel Jelloud, El Hraïria, El Kabaria, El Menzah, El Omrane, El Omrane Supérieur, El Ouardia, Ettahrir, Ezzouhour, La Goulette, La Marsa, Le Bardo, Le Kram, Medina, Séjoumi, Sidi El Béchir, Sidi Hassine

#### Ariana
- **Capital:** Ariana
- **Limits:** Borders Tunis (S), Manouba (W), Bizerte (N/NW), Ben Arous (SE); Mediterranean Sea / Gulf of Tunis (NE)
- **Delegations:** Ariana Ville, Cité Ettadhamen, Kalâat El Andalous, La Soukra, Mnihla, Raoued, Sidi Thabet

#### Ben Arous
- **Capital:** Ben Arous
- **Limits:** Borders Tunis (N), Ariana (NW), Manouba (W), Zaghouan (SW), Nabeul (SE); Gulf of Tunis / Mediterranean Sea (E)
- **Delegations:** Ben Arous, Bou Mhel El Bassatine, El Mourouj, Ezzahra, Fouchana, Hammam Chott, Hammam Lif, Medina Jedida, Mégrine, Mohamedia, Mornag, Radès

#### Manouba
- **Capital:** Manouba
- **Limits:** Borders Ariana (E), Bizerte (N), Béja (W), Zaghouan (S), Ben Arous (SE), Tunis (E)
- **Delegations:** Borj El Amri, Djedeida, Douar Hicher, El Batan, Manouba, Mornaguia, Oued Ellil, Tebourba

#### Bizerte
- **Capital:** Bizerte
- **Limits:** Borders Ariana (SE), Manouba (S), Béja (SW); Mediterranean Sea (N and E, includes La Galite islands)
- **Delegations:** Bizerte Nord, Bizerte Sud, El Alia, Ghar El Melh, Ghezala, Joumine, Mateur, Menzel Bourguiba, Menzel Jemil, Ras Jebel, Sejnane, Tinja, Utique, Zarzouna

#### Nabeul
- **Capital:** Nabeul
- **Limits:** Cap Bon peninsula. Borders Ben Arous (W), Zaghouan (SW), Sousse (S); Mediterranean Sea — Gulf of Tunis (N) and Gulf of Hammamet (E/S), includes Zembra island
- **Delegations:** Béni Khalled, Béni Khiar, Bou Argoub, Dar Châabane El Fehri, El Haouaria, El Mida, Grombalia, Hammamet, Hammam Ghezèze, Kélibia, Korba, Menzel Bouzelfa, Menzel Temime, Nabeul, Soliman, Takelsa

#### Zaghouan
- **Capital:** Zaghouan
- **Limits:** Borders Manouba/Béja (N), Ben Arous (NE), Nabeul (E), Sousse (SE), Kairouan (SW), Siliana (W)
- **Delegations:** Bir Mcherga, El Fahs, Nadhour, Saouaf, Zaghouan, Zriba

### North West

#### Béja
- **Capital:** Béja

#### Jendouba
- **Capital:** Jendouba

- **Delegations:** Aïn Draham, Balta-Bou Aouane, Bou Salem, Fernana, Ghardimaou, Jendouba, Jendouba Nord, Oued Meliz, Tabarka

#### Kef

#### Siliana
- **Capital:** Siliana

- **Delegations:** Bargou, Bou Arada, El Aroussa, El Krib, Gaâfour, Kesra, Makthar, Rouhia, Sidi Bou Rouis, Siliana Nord, Siliana Sud

### Centre East

- **Capital:** Sousse
- **Limits:** Borders Nabeul (N), Zaghouan (NW), Kairouan (W), Monastir (S), Mahdia (SW); Mediterranean Sea (E)

#### Monastir
- **Capital:** Monastir

- **Delegations:** Bekalta, Bembla, Beni Hassen, Jemmal, Ksar Hellal, Ksibet El Médiouni, Moknine, Monastir, Ouerdanine, Sahline, Sayada-Lamta-Bouhjar, Téboulba, Zéramdine

#### Mahdia
- **Capital:** Mahdia

- **Delegations:** Bou Merdes, Chebba, Chorbane, El Bradâa, El Jem, Essouassi, Hebira, Ksour Essef, Mahdia, Melloulèche, Ouled Chamekh, Rejiche, Sidi Alouane

#### Sfax
- **Capital:** Sfax
- **Limits:** Borders Mahdia (N), Kairouan (NW), Sidi Bouzid (W), Gabès (S); Mediterranean Sea / Gulf of Gabès (E), includes Kerkennah Islands
- **Delegations:** Agareb, Bir Ali Ben Khalifa, El Amra, El Hencha, Graïba, Jebiniana, Kerkennah, Mahrès, Menzel Chaker, Sakiet Eddaïer, Sakiet Ezzit, Sfax Ouest, Sfax Sud, Sfax Ville, Skhira, Thyna

### South East

#### Gabès
- **Capital:** Gabès
- **Limits:** Borders Sfax (N), Sidi Bouzid (NW), Kebili (W), Medenine (S); Mediterranean Sea / Gulf of Gabès (E)
- **Delegations:** Dkhilet Toujane, El Hamma, Gabès Médina, Gabès Ouest, Gabès Sud, Ghannouch, Habib Thameur Bouatouch, Mareth, Matmata, Menzel El Habib, Métouia, Nouvelle Matmata, Oudhref

#### Medenine
- **Capital:** Medenine
- **Limits:** Borders Gabès (N), Kebili (W), Tataouine (S); **Libya** border crossing at Ras Jedir (SE); Mediterranean Sea (E), includes Djerba island
- **Delegations:** Ben Gardane, Beni Khedache, Djerba Ajim, Djerba Houmt Souk, Djerba Midoun, Médenine Nord, Médenine Sud, Sidi Makhlouf, Zarzis

#### Tataouine
- **Capital:** Tataouine
- **Limits:** Borders Medenine (N), Kebili (W); **Libya** (S and E) — Tunisia's southernmost governorate
- **Delegations:** Beni Mehira, Bir Lahmar, Dehiba, Ghomrassen, Remada, Smâr, Tataouine Nord, Tataouine Sud

---

### South West

#### Gafsa
- **Capital:** Gafsa
- **Limits:** Borders **Algeria** (W), Kasserine (N), Sidi Bouzid (NE), Tozeur (SW), Kebili (SE)
- **Delegations:** Belkhir, El Guettar, El Ksar, Gafsa Nord, Gafsa Sud, Mdhila, Métlaoui, Moularès, Redeyef, Sened, Sidi Aïch, Sidi Boubaker, Zannouch

#### Tozeur
- **Capital:** Tozeur
- **Limits:** Borders **Algeria** (W), Gafsa (N/E), Kebili (SE)
- **Delegations:** Degache, El Hamma du Jérid, Hazoua, Nefta, Tamerza, Tozeur

#### Kebili
- **Capital:** Kébili
- **Limits:** Borders Tozeur (W), Gafsa (N), Sidi Bouzid (NE), Gabès (E), Medenine (SE), Tataouine (S); small stretch near **Algeria** (SW)
- **Delegations:** Douz Nord, Douz Sud, Faouar, Kébili Nord, Kébili Sud, Rjim Maatoug, Souk Lahad

## 4. Notes for AI parsing

- **Region** = Governorate (24 total). **Subregion** = Delegation (264+ total).
- Some governorates also have an unofficial "district" grouping (5 districts instituted by Decree 589/2023) used for regional development planning; this is separate from the 6-region statistical grouping above.
- Delegation names are transliterated from Arabic; French-style transliterations (as used in official Tunisian sources) are used here since they are most common in international datasets.
- "Limits" describe adjacency to other governorates, international borders (Algeria, Libya), or coastline (Mediterranean Sea) — not precise GPS boundary coordinates.
- Source data: Tunisian governorate/delegation structure (INS 2024 census figures) and standard geographic adjacency.
